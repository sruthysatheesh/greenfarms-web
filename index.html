Who We Are

A living farm,
a thriving community.

We are Greenera Farms — a collective of farmers, families, and food-lovers dedicated to growing the cleanest, most nourishing food in harmony with nature. Every seed we plant is an act of hope.

Our farm spans acres of sun-drenched fields where heritage varieties grow slowly, naturally, and abundantly — the way they were always meant to.

The Model

Farming together,
harvesting abundance.

Our community model puts people at the heart of agriculture. Members get weekly harvest boxes, farm access, seasonal workshops, and a real stake in where their food comes from.

Seasonal Harvest

Fresh produce delivered weekly — picked at peak ripeness, never a day too early.

Community Shares

Flexible membership plans that let you own a share of the harvest season.

Zero Waste Farming

Composting, water harvesting, and natural pest management guide every decision.

Summer Workshops

Learn fermentation, seed saving, natural cooking, and the rhythms of the land.

Farm Features

Designed by
nature, refined
by care.

  • Over 40 heritage fruit and vegetable varieties
  • Naturally irrigated with rainwater harvesting
  • No synthetic pesticides or fertilisers — ever
  • Pollinator-friendly hedgerows and wildflower meadows
  • Solar-powered cold storage and processing units
