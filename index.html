Who We Are

A living farm,
a thriving community.

We are Greenera Farms, a climate-resilient community farm which is a self-reliant and self-sustaining ecosystem where people come together to build resilient infrastructure, regenerate natural resources, and adopt a climate-adaptive lifestyle in harmony with nature.

At its core, it is about caring for the soil, growing healthy food, supporting people and communities, and creating a resilient future.

The Model

Individually owned,
collectively managed.

The project follows a hybrid ownership model, where farm spaces are individually registered, while infrastructure, development, and maintenance are shared and collectively managed.

Why Does It Exist?

To respond to:

  • Rising temperatures
  • Urban disconnection
  • Unhealthy lifestyles
  • Loss of ecological connection

What Makes It Different?

It offers:

  • Individually registered farm spaces
  • Shared ecosystem infrastructure
  • Cooler microclimate zones
  • Sustainable community experience

What Benefit Does It Create?

It delivers:

  • Abundant natural resources
  • Healthier environment
  • Future-ready green spaces
  • Peaceful and happy life

Who Is It For?

Made for:

  • Urban dwellers
  • Families without farmlands
  • Budget-friendly investors
  • People with a love for nature

Farm Features

Where soil, nature and people thrive.

  • Over 40 heritage fruit and vegetable varieties
  • Naturally irrigated with rainwater harvesting
  • No synthetic pesticides or fertilisers — ever
  • Pollinator-friendly hedgerows and wildflower meadows
  • Solar-powered cold storage and processing units
